# AI Restaurant Battle - Complete Tutorial

Welcome to AI Restaurant Battle, an exciting real-time strategy game where you compete against five AI-controlled restaurants to dominate the local food market!

## 🎯 Game Overview

**Objective**: Build the most successful restaurant by the end of 7 days
**Duration**: 7 minutes real-time (1 minute per game day)
**Competition**: You vs 5 AI restaurants with different strategies
**Victory**: Achieve the highest final score based on market share, revenue, satisfaction, and efficiency

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Your Starting Position
- **Cash**: $50,000 to manage your restaurant
- **Menu**: 3 basic items (Classic Burger $12.99, Caesar Salad $8.99, Grilled Chicken $15.99)
- **Staff**: 1 Head Chef and 1 Server
- **Equipment**: Basic kitchen setup
- **Market Share**: 16.67% (equal starting position)

### Understanding the Interface

**Top Bar (Game Header)**:
- **Day Counter**: Shows current day (Day 1/7)
- **Timer**: Countdown for current day (60 seconds)
- **Revenue Chart**: Real-time performance graph
- **Ranking**: Live market share leaderboard

**Main Game Area**:
- **Restaurant View**: Visual representation of your restaurant
- **Customer Animation**: See customers entering, ordering, eating, and leaving
- **Your Stats**: Cash, customer count, satisfaction percentage

**Right Sidebar (AI Assistant)**:
- **Sakura AI**: Your strategic advisor
- **Advice Panel**: Real-time tips and warnings
- **Market Analysis**: Competitive intelligence

**Bottom Panel (Controls)**:
- **Menu Management**: Add, modify, and remove dishes
- **Marketing**: Launch advertising campaigns
- **Operations**: Hire staff and upgrade equipment

## 🎮 How to Play

### Phase 1: Early Game Strategy (Days 1-2)

**Focus on Foundation Building**:

1. **Monitor Your Finances**
   - Watch your cash flow carefully
   - Daily expenses: ~$2,500 (rent, utilities, staff)
   - Keep at least $10,000 in reserve

2. **Optimize Your Menu**
   - Click "Add New Dish" to create profitable items
   - **Golden Rule**: Price items at 2.5-3x their cost
   - Example: If a dish costs $5 to make, price it at $12.50-$15
   - Remove items with low popularity (under 40%)

3. **Build Customer Base**
   - Launch small marketing campaigns ($2,000-$5,000)
   - Focus on maintaining 75%+ satisfaction
   - Watch competitor pricing and stay competitive

### Phase 2: Growth Phase (Days 3-5)

**Scale Your Operations**:

1. **Staff Management**
   - Hire additional staff when cash > $15,000
   - **Chefs**: Improve cooking speed and food quality
   - **Waiters**: Enhance customer service and satisfaction
   - Balance: 2-3 chefs, 2-3 waiters optimal

2. **Advanced Menu Strategy**
   - Add variety: 6-8 menu items ideal
   - Quick items (5-10 second cook time) for high turnover
   - Premium items ($20+) for higher profit margins
   - Monitor item performance and adjust prices

3. **Marketing Expansion**
   - Launch bigger campaigns ($8,000-$15,000)
   - Marketing effect: ~1% satisfaction boost per $1,000 spent
   - Campaign benefits last 3 days

### Phase 3: Market Domination (Days 6-7)

**Push for Victory**:

1. **Aggressive Growth**
   - Invest heavily in marketing if you have the cash
   - Price optimization: Monitor competitor moves
   - Equipment upgrades for efficiency boosts

2. **Competitive Response**
   - Watch AI strategies and counter them
   - If AI undercuts prices, improve quality or service
   - Focus on customer satisfaction to build loyalty

## 🤖 Understanding Your AI Competitors

### Fire Grill (Aggressive AI)
- **Strategy**: Undercuts prices by 20%
- **Behavior**: Heavy marketing spending, frequent menu changes
- **Counter**: Focus on quality and service over price wars
- **Warning Signs**: Rapid market share growth, low prices

### Wisdom Table (Strategic AI)
- **Strategy**: Data-driven decisions, optimal pricing
- **Behavior**: Removes bad items, calculated moves
- **Counter**: Stay innovative, update menu frequently
- **Warning Signs**: Steady growth, efficient operations

### Hospitality House (Emotional AI)
- **Strategy**: Premium pricing, customer satisfaction focus
- **Behavior**: High service investment, quality over quantity
- **Counter**: Match their service levels or beat on price
- **Warning Signs**: High satisfaction scores, loyal customers

### Lightning Kitchen (Speed AI)
- **Strategy**: Fast service, quick menu items
- **Behavior**: Equipment upgrades, efficiency optimization
- **Counter**: Offer variety they can't match quickly
- **Warning Signs**: High customer turnover, speed focus

### Harmony Restaurant (Balanced AI)
- **Strategy**: Conservative, well-rounded approach
- **Behavior**: Steady growth, market-average pricing
- **Counter**: Take risks they won't, be more aggressive
- **Warning Signs**: Consistent performance across all metrics

## 💡 Advanced Tips & Strategies

### Financial Management
- **Emergency Fund**: Always keep $5,000+ for unexpected expenses
- **ROI Focus**: Marketing should return 2x investment in increased revenue
- **Cost Control**: Monitor daily expenses vs. daily revenue

### Menu Optimization
- **Sweet Spot Pricing**: Items between $8-$18 typically perform best
- **Cook Time Balance**: Mix quick items (5s) with profitable slow items (20s)
- **Seasonal Adjustments**: Add trendy items, remove stagnant ones

### Marketing Mastery
- **Timing**: Launch campaigns at day start for maximum effect
- **Budget Strategy**: Spend 10-20% of available cash on marketing
- **Competitive Marketing**: Counter AI campaigns with your own

### Operational Excellence
- **Staff Ratios**: Maintain 1:1 or 2:3 chef-to-waiter ratio
- **Equipment Priority**: Kitchen upgrades give best efficiency boost
- **Capacity Planning**: Hire staff before reaching capacity limits

## 📊 Scoring System Explained

Your final score combines multiple factors:

- **Market Share** (40% weight): Percentage of customers you capture
- **Total Revenue** (30% weight): Money earned over 7 days
- **Customer Satisfaction** (20% weight): Service quality rating
- **Operational Efficiency** (10% weight): Revenue-to-expense ratio

**Pro Tip**: A balanced approach usually beats specialization!

## 🎯 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Over-expansion**: Hiring too much staff too quickly
2. **Price Wars**: Competing solely on lowest prices
3. **Marketing Neglect**: Not investing in customer acquisition
4. **Menu Bloat**: Having too many similar items
5. **Cash Burning**: Spending without considering daily expenses
6. **Ignoring Satisfaction**: Focusing only on revenue
7. **Static Strategy**: Not adapting to AI competitor moves

## 🎲 Random Events Guide

**Positive Events**:
- **Local Festival**: +50% customers for 1 day
- **TV Feature**: +30% satisfaction for 2 days

**Negative Events**:
- **Rainy Day**: -30% customers for 1 day  
- **Price Spike**: +30% food costs for 2 days

**Strategy**: Save cash for opportunities, weather negative events
