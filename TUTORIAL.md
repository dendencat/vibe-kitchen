# AI Restaurant Battle — Tutorial / チュートリアル

Your goal: **the highest market share when day 7 ends**. Each day is 60
seconds. Market share is your share of *all guests served so far*, so early
leads matter — but there are seven days to catch up.

目標は「7日目終了時の市場シェア1位」。シェアはこれまでに提供した全来客数に占める割合です。

## How guests choose a restaurant / お客様の選び方

Every second, the plaza's guests are split between the six shops by
**attractiveness**:

- **Price** — cheaper than the market average pulls more guests.
- **Satisfaction** — weighs heavily; happy guests bring more guests.
- **Buzz** — from marketing campaigns; fades over time.
- **Variety** — more dishes (up to ~7) attract more kinds of guests.
- **Popularity** of your dishes — good value and high ratings raise it.

Then **capacity** decides how many you can actually serve. When demand
exceeds capacity, guests walk away (grey in 3D) and satisfaction drops.
Demand peaks at the **lunch** and **dinner** rushes (white markers on the
day bar).

## Day 1 checklist / 1日目にやること

1. **Add 2–3 dishes** (Menu → *+ Add dish* → pick a preset). Five or more
   dishes is a good target.
2. **Check prices** against Sakura's advice — if she says you are above the
   market, trim a few dishes with **−**.
3. Watch the **Load** meter (Operations). If it goes above 100 % at lunch,
   hire a **chef** (more throughput) or a **waiter** (throughput + happier
   guests).

## Marketing / マーケティング

- **Social media** ($2,000) is the most cost-effective way to keep buzz up.
- Buzz fades ~1.5 % per second, so **launch before a rush** rather than right
  after it, and **space campaigns out** instead of stacking them.
- **TV** ($12,000) is a big swing — great on the last days or to answer a
  rival that is pulling away.

## Reading your rivals / ライバルの読み方

Click a shop (or a ranking row) to see its strategy, prices, staff and buzz.

| Rival | Watch out for | Counter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fire Grill | Very low prices, big ad bursts, may run low on cash | Don't race to the bottom — win on satisfaction and variety |
| Wisdom Table | Steady, efficient pricing | Out-market it; it spends cautiously |
| Hospitality House | High satisfaction, premium prices | Undercut on price |
| Lightning Kitchen | Huge throughput, cheap fast food | Make sure *your* capacity isn't the bottleneck |
| Harmony Restaurant | Average at everything | Specialise: price + buzz |

## Mid and late game / 中盤・終盤

- Keep an eye on **cash** — campaigns and hires are investments, but salaries
  and rent are charged every second.
- Upgrade the **kitchen** once you are regularly full at rush hour.
- On **day 7**, spend: unused cash does not score, but share does.

## Scoring / スコア

`share × 1000 + revenue × 0.1 + satisfaction × 100 + (revenue ÷ expenses) × 50`

The winner is decided by market share; the score and the four awards
(revenue, satisfaction, efficiency, innovation) are shown on the results
screen.

## Difficulty / 難易度

On **Easy** an active player should win; **Normal** needs good timing;
**Hard** rivals pull ~20 % more guests than you at equal footing.
